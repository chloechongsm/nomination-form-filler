# Nomination Form Filler

A single-page web app that fills the Great Eastern "Appointment / Change of Nominee(s) / Trustee(s)" form (NBZ-FNOMI-V07-082025) from customer details, and exports the filled form as PDF.

- Add customers from CUSTOMER SERVICE printouts (PDF), an Excel/CSV list, or by hand
- Dropdowns for each answer; a "Missing info" Excel list you can fill in and upload back
- Download each filled form, or all forms in one PDF

**Privacy:** everything runs inside your browser. Files you add are never uploaded anywhere, and nothing is saved after you close the page (use "Save customer list" to keep your work).

Signatures, witnessing and page 5 consent are left blank on purpose and must be completed by hand.
